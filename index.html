Hermes Agent is a personal AI assistant that runs on its owner's own computer. It is not a public product and it does not offer sign-up. The only Google account it connects to is the owner's.

What it does with Google

When the owner asks, the assistant reads and manages his Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets and Google Contacts. Typical tasks are sorting and answering email, adding reminders and meetings to the calendar, and finding or editing files.

Access goes straight from the owner's machine to Google's APIs with an OAuth client that he controls. No other company's server sits in the middle.
